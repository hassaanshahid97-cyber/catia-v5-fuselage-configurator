FEATURES:
==========

BuildSolidFuselage builds a complete Findus UAV fuselage:

STAGE 1: Connect & Create Part
  - Attaches to running CATIA V5
  - Creates new Part document with factories

STAGE 2: Bulkhead Splines (OML)
  - 4 closed elliptical splines at key X stations
  - Defines the outer mold line (OML)
  - Used as loft sections

STAGE 3: Outer-Skin Solid (PartBody)
  - Loft surface through 4 bulkheads
  - Nose fill (X=150)
  - Tail fill (X=1400)
  - Join surfaces into closed shell
  - Close surface to create SOLID body

STAGE 4: 4 Longeron Solids
  - Rounded-rectangle cross-section (12x12 mm with R2 fillets)
  - Located at 4 corners of fuselage (TP, TS, BP, BS)
  - Extends from X=200 to X=1100 (5 stations)
  - Each in its own Body for independent visibility control

ENGINEERING NOTES:
===================

Fuselage Design Rationale:
  - Closed-cell construction carries shear & torsion (Bredt-Batho theory)
  - 4 corner longerons provide bending stiffness in pitch & yaw
  - Longerons skip nose zone (motor mount) and tail zone (battery)
  - Rounded rectangles reduce stress concentration vs sharp corners

CATIA API Notes:
  - Late-bound COM calls (Option Strict Off)
  - Uses HybridShapeFactory (GSD) for splines, lofts, fills, joins
  - Uses ShapeFactory (Part Design) for CloseSurface solid
  - Property assignment for Join attributes (NOT SetConnex/SetManifold)
  - Graceful degradation: surfaces OK even if CloseSurface fails
